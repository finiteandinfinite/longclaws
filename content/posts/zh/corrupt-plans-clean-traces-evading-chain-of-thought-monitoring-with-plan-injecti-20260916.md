# Corrupt Plans, Clean Traces: Evading Chain-of-Thought Monitoring with Plan Injection

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15989v1)

作者: Keertana Chidambaram, Andrew Ilyas, Vasilis Syrgkanis

Chain-of-thought (CoT) monitoring is a safety strategy where the reasoning of a large language model "actor" is inspected by a "monitor" (often another language model) for signs of unsafe planning, deception, or misalignment. We find that planting harmful but benign-sounding reasoning in the actor's context can steer it to perform adversarial actions while evading monitors, an attack we term "plan injection".
