# Sample-efficient prediction of high-entropy alloy strength through outcome-aware robust sequential learning

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18462v1)

作者: Ahmed Shoyeb Raihan, Md Habibor Rahman, Ashish Nimbarte 等

Accurate prediction of high-entropy alloy (HEA) strength can guide composition and processing decisions; however, obtaining tensile-test data for HEAs can be costly and experimentally demanding. This study proposes Robust Outcome-aware Sequential Experimentation for High-Entropy Alloys (ROSE-HEA), a sample-efficient framework for predicting ultimate tensile strength (UTS) from alloy composition and processing conditions.
