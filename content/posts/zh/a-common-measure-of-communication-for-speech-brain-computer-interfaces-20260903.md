# A Common Measure of Communication for Speech Brain-Computer Interfaces

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02887v1)

作者: Dulhan Jayalath, Benjamin Ballyk, Oiwi Parker Jones

Speech brain-computer interfaces (speech BCIs) translate neural activity into language, offering a path towards restoring speech for people with paralysis and, more broadly, enabling new forms of natural human-computer interaction. Despite this promise, the field lacks a common measure of progress because systems use different datasets, recording methods, types of speech, and vocabularies, so their reported scores are rarely comparable.
