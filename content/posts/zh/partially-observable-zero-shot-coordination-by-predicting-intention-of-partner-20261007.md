# Partially Observable Zero-shot coordination by Predicting Intention of Partner

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08142v1)

作者: Jinnyeong Yang, Yuhwan Jeong, Hoyong Kwon 等

Zero-shot coordination in embodied settings requires acting while the partner is intermittently out of view, leaving existing methods with ambiguous partner representations and uncertainty over hidden partner states. We propose Predicting Intention of Partner (PIP) to jointly address these challenges. PIP uses a Joint-view VAE to distill richer training-time evidence from the union of both agents' local observations into a partner representation available from local observations alone. Partner-s
