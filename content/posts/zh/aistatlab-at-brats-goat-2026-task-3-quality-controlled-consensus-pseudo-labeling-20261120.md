# AIstatLab at BraTS-GoAT 2026 Task 3: Quality-Controlled Consensus Pseudo-Labeling and Dense Expert Adaptation

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25477v1)

作者: Heeseung Shin, Changwon Lim

BraTS-GoAT Task 3 evaluates brain-tumor segmentation across heterogeneous entities using shared ET, TC, and WT regions. We present a 3D ResidualEncoderUNet trained with quality-controlled consensus pseudo-labels, a training-only nested-region objective, and a four-expert bottleneck adapter. Five-fold ResEnc-M/L teachers gener ated predictions for 1,138 unlabeled cases; fusion, hierarchy enforcement, and confidence-and-agreement screening retained 983 for training with 1,351 annotated cases.
