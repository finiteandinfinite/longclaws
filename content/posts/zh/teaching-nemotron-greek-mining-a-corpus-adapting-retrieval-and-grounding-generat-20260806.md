> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05138v1)

作者: Ayoub Kirouane, Christos Petrocheilos

Modern Greek is absent from NVIDIA's Nemotron retrieval models and from major multilingual retrieval benchmarks, despite being important for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) in legal, energy, financial, and medical applications.
