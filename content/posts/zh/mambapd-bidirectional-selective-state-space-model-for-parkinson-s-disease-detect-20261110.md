# MambaPD: Bidirectional Selective State Space Model for Parkinson's Disease Detection from Resting-State EEG

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12026v1)

作者: Ayesha Zulfiqar

Typical diagnosis of Parkinson's disease (PD) occurs only after significant dopaminergic loss, while resting-state EEG abnormalities like alpha slowing and reduced coherence occur earlier. While many published EEG-based PD detectors claim accuracy greater than 95%, many use epoch-level splits which leak subject identity.
