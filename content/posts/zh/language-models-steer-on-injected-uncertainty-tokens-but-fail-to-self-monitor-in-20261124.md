# Language Models Steer on Injected Uncertainty Tokens, but Fail to Self-Monitor In Situ

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31228v1)

作者: Daniel Theron

Prompting language models to verbalize stepwise numerical confidence during generation is an increasingly popular approach for triggering backtracking and error correction in agentic reasoning. This strategy presupposes two capabilities: (1) in-situ monitoring fidelity (does confidence drop when an error occurs?) and (2) downstream steering control (does an uncertainty token causally steer the model to repair its error?).
