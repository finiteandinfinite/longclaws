# BrickBench: Evaluating Agentic Brick Design

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12452v1)

作者: Peter Kulits, Yiqing Xu, R. Kenny Jones 等

We propose BrickBench, a benchmark for agentic text-conditioned LEGO-set design. Given a prompt, an agent is tasked with producing an assembly that not only satisfies semantic and design criteria, but that can also be physically built. To do so, it must select parts from a discrete library and reason jointly about local and global constraints.
