> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18553v1)

作者: Aayush Marasini, Barsat Khadka, Nick Rahimi

Recent work shows that large language models (LLMs) can operate directly on the parameters of other neural networks. They can read what a network was trained to do, fix a network that fails, and write working networks from scratch, all without gradient descent. This capability has been termed Weight-Space Programming (WSP). Exploring this new paradigm systematically requires a robust benchmark.
