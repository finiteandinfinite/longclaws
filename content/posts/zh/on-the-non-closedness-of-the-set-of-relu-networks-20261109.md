# On the (non-)closedness of the set of ReLU networks

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09887v1)

作者: Eloi Martinet, Leon Bungert

We prove that the set $\mathcal{N}_N$ of ReLU networks with one hidden layer and at most $N\in\mathbb N$ neurons is weakly closed in $W^{1,p}(Ω)$, for a bounded connected open set $Ω\subset\mathbb R^d$. Consequently, every coercive, weakly lower semicontinuous energy on this space attains its minimum over $\mathcal{N}_N$.
