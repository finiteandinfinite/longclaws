# How to Turn an Attention Neural Network into an Interpretable Hard-Logic Predicate Circuit: Truth-Table Predicate Extraction and State-Machine Merging under Ship-of-Theseus Replacement

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24381v1)

作者: Yuchen Li, Zongqi Fan, Xintong Li 等

We convert a trained attention network as a whole, attention included, into a hard-logic predicate circuit that runs in its place. Every unit of the circuit is a 0/1 predicate on tokens, and every map between predicates is a minimised truth table. Short tables, such as the product of a query code and a key code, are minimised exactly, and wide feature-to-feature transitions become implication rules by noise-tolerant minimisation.
