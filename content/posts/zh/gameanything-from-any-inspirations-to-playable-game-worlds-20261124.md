# GameAnything: From Any Inspirations to Playable Game Worlds

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29238v1)

作者: Wenyi Wu, Siqi Liu, Yuru Feng 等

AI-driven game development holds the potential to transform diverse human inspirations into interactive experiences. However, existing systems and benchmarks primarily focus on explicit development specifications, with limited support for adapting multimodal references and evaluating actual gameplay experiences. We introduce GameAnything, an open-source agentic platform that transforms multimodal inspirations into playable extensions of existing games through iterative development. GameAnything
