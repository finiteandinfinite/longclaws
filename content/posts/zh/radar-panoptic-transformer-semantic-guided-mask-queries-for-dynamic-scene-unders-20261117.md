# Radar Panoptic Transformer: Semantic-Guided Mask Queries for Dynamic Scene Understanding

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20203v1)

作者: Mayank Mayank, Bharanidhar Duraisamy, Rohit Mohan 等

Automotive radar provides long-range sensing and direct Doppler velocity measurements, but its sparse and non-uniformly sampled point clouds pose significant challenges for panoptic segmentation. Dynamic objects often yield only a few detections amid a dominant static background, providing limiting the geometric cues for separating object instances.
