# Graded convolutional networks: scale invariance without the scale and robustness to unknown blur

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09878v1)

作者: Tanush Shaska

The Gaussian jet of an image is a graded vector space: a dilation by $λ$ multiplies derivatives of order $k$ by $λ^{-k}$. Local graded convolutions are exactly homogeneous differential operators, and a local feature is dilation-invariant if and only if it factors through the scale-normalized jet or, without the scale, through the retraction onto the weighted sphere of the grading.
