# TowerBridge: Bridging the Dual-Encoder Modality Gap via Weight-Space Programming

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31233v1)

作者: Hani Alomari, Md Atabuzzaman, Chris Thomas

CLIP-style dual encoders map images and text through two separate towers into a shared embedding space, where the two modalities occupy distinct regions separated by a modality gap. Retrieval fails when wrong matches, or hard negatives, outrank the correct one, and the gap is the usual suspect. Yet removing it post hoc degrades retrieval on every CLIP checkpoint we test.
