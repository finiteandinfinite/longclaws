# PhysStream: Streaming Physics-Grounded Video Generation with Structured Scene Memory and Fine-Grained Motion Control

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17521v1)

作者: Chuhao Chen, Peter Wonka, Chaoyang Wang 等

Interactive control for video generation is moving from coarse prompts toward fine-grained, physically meaningful manipulation of dynamic scenes. Yet existing controllable methods either require the full control schedule before generation starts, or use pixel-space signals that dictate object positions rather than physical dynamics.
