# An Optimal Agnostic PAC Algorithm

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06363v1)

作者: Markus Engelund Mathiasen, Jian Qian, Nikita Zhivotovskiy

Let $H\subseteq\{-1,+1\}^X$ be a class of finite VC dimension $d\ge1$. Writing $L$ for the binary risk and $L^*=\min_{h\in H}L(h)$, we construct a learner achieving the statistically optimal risk bound: from an i.i.d.\ sample of size $n$, for every $0&lt;δ\le 1/2$, with probability at least $1-δ$, \[ L(\widehat h) \le L^*+ 7\cdot10^8\left( \sqrt{\frac{L^*(d+\log(1/δ))}{n}} +\frac{d+\log(1/δ)}{n} \right). \] This settles the sample complexity of agnostic PAC learning up to universal constants at
