# Robots in the City: Persona-Based Perceptions of Urban Robots

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05954v1)

作者: Keonhee Jang, Kanghoon Lee, Seokwoo Yun 等

Pedestrians increasingly encounter robots in urban public spaces without interacting with them, but how robots change the perceived safety of a street remains unclear. We examine how robot presence and type affect perceived safety across urban contexts. We generate 18 images of Seoul streets that vary in time of day, road class, and robot condition (no robot, a delivery robot, or a humanoid).
