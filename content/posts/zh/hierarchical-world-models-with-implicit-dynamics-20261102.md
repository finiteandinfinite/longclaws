# Hierarchical World Models with Implicit Dynamics

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42146v1)

作者: Gaoyue Zhou, Yvonne Wu, Zichen Jeff Cui 等

Planning with world models enables test-time adaptation for embodied agents, offering superior generalization over direct policy learning. However, long-horizon tasks remain hindered by cumulative errors and search complexity. Existing hierarchical methods often use rigid macro-actions or latent skills, leading to brittle representations and limited fine-grained control.
