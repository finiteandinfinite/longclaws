# Lévy Attention: Single-Pass Predictive Uncertainty for Continuous-Time Attention

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19171v1)

作者: Sotirios P. Chatzis, Loukas Papadoulas

Deep models for irregularly-sampled time series answer queries at arbitrary continuous timestamps, yet report nothing about how far each answer should be trusted. We show the attention layer itself can close that gap: with the right stochastic formulation, the pass that makes each prediction also reports, in closed form and at no extra cost, how far it should be trusted. We introduce Lévy Attention, a cross-attention operator whose output is a stochastic integral against an inhomogeneous Poisson
