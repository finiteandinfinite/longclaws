> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09928v1)

作者: Hunar Batra, Lachin Naghashyar, Ashkan Khakzar 等

Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) exhibit strong visual understanding, yet the internal features that cause these behaviors remain difficult to identify, audit, or control.
