> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32969v1)

作者: Amber Xie, Jiawei Fu, Xiaofeng Guo 等

Collecting high-quality demonstrations remains a central bottleneck for learning dexterous manipulation. Teleoperation records executable robot actions, but it requires a complete robot setup and places the burden of coordinating many finger joints on the operator.
