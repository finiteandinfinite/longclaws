# The Theoretical Foundation of Socratic Tests: Dynamic, Multimodal, Conversational Examinations

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29624v1)

作者: Ilya Mikhelson

Traditional static assessments rely on a subtractive, deficit-based grading model that often penalizes ambition and obscures diagnostic feedback. Conversely, traditional face-to-face oral examinations introduce severe construct-irrelevant variance by exacerbating performative anxiety and the sociological power imbalances inherent to academic hierarchies.
