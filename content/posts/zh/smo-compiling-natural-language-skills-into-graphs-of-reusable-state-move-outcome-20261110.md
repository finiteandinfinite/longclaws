# SMO: Compiling Natural Language Skills into Graphs of Reusable State-Move-Outcome Bricks

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12041v1)

作者: Chao Chen, Jiashuo Zhang, Yunhang Shen 等

Large language model agents increasingly rely on reusable natural-language skills, yet growing skill collections remain accumulations of isolated text artifacts that are difficult to compare, deduplicate, and compose. We argue that the reusable content of a skill lies not in its surface wording but in its procedural transitions: which operation to execute under a given state and which observable outcome to produce.
