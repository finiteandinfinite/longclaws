# Agentic Detection of Online Conspiracies

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30250v1)

作者: Lior Biton, Oren Tsur

The same surface content may express endorsement, legitimate concerns, criticism, satire, or mockery.
