# What Majority Labels Hide: Annotator Disagreement and Model Errors in Multi-Dialect Arabic Sexism Detection

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24383v1)

作者: Alaa Bazaid, Arkaitz Zubiaga
