# From Classification to Regression: Using a Fruitfly to Solve Equations

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27196v1)

作者: Shady E. Ahmed, Panos Stinis

We present a novel approach to regression tasks using classification which is motivated by the mechanism used by fruitflies to sense their environment. Specifically, we formulate a general framework for learning nonlinear input-output relationships by replacing complex global surrogate models with a finite library of representative local patterns.
