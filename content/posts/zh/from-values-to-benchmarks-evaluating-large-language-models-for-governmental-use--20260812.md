# From Values to Benchmarks: Evaluating Large Language Models for Governmental Use in Dutch

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09925v1)

作者: Laurens Samson, Iva Gornishka, Gossa Lô 等

Large language models are increasingly being deployed in governmental settings, yet few existing evaluation frameworks jointly reflect the values of public administration and the linguistic requirements of non-English contexts. We present the "Grip on LLMs" framework, a systematic evaluation suite for Dutch governmental use developed in collaboration with domain experts from a major Dutch municipal organisation.
