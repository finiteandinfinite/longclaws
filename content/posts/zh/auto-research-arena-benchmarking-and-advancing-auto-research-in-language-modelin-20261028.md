> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35866v1)

作者: Xi Chen, Chongjian Ge, Yiran Xu 等

As autonomous research systems become more capable, benchmarks must both evaluate their performance and support meaningful research progress. We introduce Auto-Research Arena, a multi-target benchmark for evaluating autonomous research systems and discovering ideas in language modeling.
