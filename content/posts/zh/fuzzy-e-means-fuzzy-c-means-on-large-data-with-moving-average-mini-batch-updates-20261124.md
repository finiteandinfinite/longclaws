# Fuzzy e-Means: Fuzzy c-Means on Large Data with Moving-Average Mini-Batch Updates

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29241v1)

作者: Paritosh Tiwari, Punit Rathore

Fuzzy c-means (FCM) gives every point a degree of membership in every cluster. Its standard solver, alternating optimization (AO), needs a full pass over the data per iteration and often many iterations, while methods built for large data stop at a worse objective or still need tens of passes. We run FCM with the optimizer of e-means, a recent mini-batch k-means that keeps moving averages of the number and the sum of the points in each cluster and sets its batch size and step size by scaling law
