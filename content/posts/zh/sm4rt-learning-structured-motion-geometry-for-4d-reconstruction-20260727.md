# SM4RT: Learning Structured Motion Geometry for 4D Reconstruction

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22534v1)

作者: Shing Ho J. Lin, Wenzhao Zheng, Dong Zhuo 等

Geometry Foundation Models (GFMs) have substantially advanced monocular 3D reconstruction, yet extending this capability to 4D dynamic understanding remains a fundamental challenge. Most existing motion perception methods (e.g., sparse tracking, dense point-wise flow) treat motion as independent point-wise displacements, ignoring the structured nature of physical motion.
