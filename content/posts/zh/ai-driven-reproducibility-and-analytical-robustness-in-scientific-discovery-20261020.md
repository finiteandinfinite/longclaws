# AI-Driven Reproducibility and Analytical Robustness in Scientific Discovery

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23845v1)

作者: Shicheng Liu, Krista Opsahl-Ong, Andrew Drozdov 等

We investigate whether LLM agents can help automate reproduction and robustness analysis, using a medical case study grounded in the Surveillance, Epidemiology, and End Results (SEER) database, a widely used U.S. cancer registry underlying tens of thousands of published studies.
