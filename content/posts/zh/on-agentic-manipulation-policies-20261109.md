# On Agentic Manipulation Policies

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09892v1)

作者: Chen Chen, Omid Bagherieh, Naveen Nikamanth Appiah Balaji 等

Recent work on robot manipulation has focused on specialist policies trained on demonstrations. We show that generalist AI models can control manipulators more reliably than specialist policies such as vision-language-action models (VLAs) and world-action models (WAMs). We systematically analyze agentic manipulation policies (AMPs), in which a generalist AI model commands the manipulator directly, adapting its actions as the task unfolds.
