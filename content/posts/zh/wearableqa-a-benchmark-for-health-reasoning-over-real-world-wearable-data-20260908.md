# WearableQA: A Benchmark for Health Reasoning over Real-World Wearable Data

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05405v1)

作者: Ji Soo Lee, Xilun Chen, Pierce Chuang 等

Recent advances in wearable sensing enable continuous monitoring of physiological and behavioral signals, yet existing benchmarks rarely evaluate whether AI systems can reason over a real user's longitudinal wearable record. We introduce WearableQA, a benchmark comprising 4,084 10-option multiple-choice questions constructed from the wearable time series, blood biomarkers, and demographics of 200 real users, each with up to 500 days of daily measurements.
