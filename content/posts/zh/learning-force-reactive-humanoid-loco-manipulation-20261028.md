> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35862v1)

作者: Sihang Li, Xiaofeng Guo, Jinkun Cao 等

On a humanoid, this force must often be coordinated across both hands and the whole body, and cannot be specified by kinematic trajectories alone. Therefore, we present FLORE, a framework for learning force-reactive humanoid loco-manipulation.
