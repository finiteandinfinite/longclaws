# Q-based Variational Inverse Reinforcement Learning

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16888v1)

作者: Ondrej Bajgar, Peter Tisnikar, Alessandro Abate 等

The development of safe and beneficial AI requires that systems can learn and act in accordance with human preferences. However, explicitly specifying these preferences by hand is often infeasible. Inverse reinforcement learning (IRL) addresses this challenge by inferring preferences, represented as reward functions, from expert behaviour.
