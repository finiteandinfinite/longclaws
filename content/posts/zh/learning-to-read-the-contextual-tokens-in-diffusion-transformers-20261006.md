作者: Omer Dahary, Etai Sella, Hadar Averbuch-Elor 等

Multimodal Diffusion Transformers (MM-DiTs) jointly process visual and textual representations throughout generation. These models repeatedly update the text tokens through multimodal attention, forming dynamic contextual tokens whose function is not well understood.
