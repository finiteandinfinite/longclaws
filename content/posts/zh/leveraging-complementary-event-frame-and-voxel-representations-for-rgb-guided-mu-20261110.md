# Leveraging Complementary Event Frame and Voxel Representations for RGB-Guided Multimodal Pre-training

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12040v1)

作者: Wentao Wu, Xiao Wang, Yi Wang 等

In this paper, we propose CM3AE 2.0, a unified RGB Frame and Event-Voxel/-Frame pre-training framework. By jointly learning multimodal spatiotemporal representations and exploiting latent cross-modal dependencies through cross-modal generation, the framework enhances multimodal information interaction and collaborative modeling capabilities. Specifically, we first propose an Event-aware Spatiotemporal Masked Reconstruction (ESMR) strategy to learn unified multimodal spatiotemporal representation
