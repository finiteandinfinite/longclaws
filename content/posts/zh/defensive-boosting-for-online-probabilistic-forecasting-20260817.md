# Defensive Boosting for Online Probabilistic Forecasting

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13554v1)

作者: Georgy Noarov, Aaron Roth

We study online probabilistic forecasting of binary outcomes chosen by an adaptive adversary. Given an online learning algorithm for a weak hypothesis class $H$, we would like to efficiently obtain two incomparable guarantees that existing online boosting techniques provide separately. Online gradient boosting competes in Brier score with the best predictor induced by the span of $H$ on every sequence, but promises nothing when the span does not contain an accurate predictor.
