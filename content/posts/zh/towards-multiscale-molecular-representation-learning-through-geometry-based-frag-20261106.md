# Towards Multiscale Molecular Representation Learning through Geometry-Based Fragmentation for Polymer Property Prediction

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07998v1)

作者: Makoto Takamoto, Chaitanya Sampara, Viktor Zaverkin 等

It is well established that molecular substructures can contribute unequally to chemical and physical properties, with specific motifs or side chains often playing dominant roles. Fragment-based representations have therefore emerged as an effective strategy for incorporating chemically meaningful inductive biases, as exemplified by molecular fingerprints and fragment-based attention mechanisms. However, many existing fragmentation-based machine learning methods rely on predefined chemical rules
