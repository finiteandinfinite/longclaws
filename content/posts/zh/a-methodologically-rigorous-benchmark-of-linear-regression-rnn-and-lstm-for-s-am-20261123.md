# A Methodologically Rigorous Benchmark of Linear Regression,RNN, and LSTM for S&amp;P 500 Forecasting:Identifying Sources of Performance Inflation in Financial DeepLearning

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27978v1)

作者: Hari Sitaula, Joseph Opitz

This study examines one-day-ahead forecasting of the S&amp;P 500 closing level using a 2006-2025 dataset that includes fundamental price data, macroeconomic indicators, and engineered technical features. We benchmark three model families, namely linear regression, vanilla recurrent neural networks (RNN), and long short-term memory networks (LSTM), within a unified preprocessing and chronological evaluation pipeline. A central contribution of this work is a methodological audit of common practice
