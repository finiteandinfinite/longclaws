# Do LLMs Act on What They Know? From Partner Representations to Cooperative Actions

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08129v1)

作者: Yuhwan Jeong, Jinnyeong Yang, Kuk-Jin Yoon

Cooperation with unfamiliar partners requires adapting to communication conventions that are not known in advance. We study this problem in a controlled Hanabi-derived environment with scripted hint generation, LLM-controlled receiving decisions, and frozen model weights. Across eight LLMs, linear probes recover intent conventions substantially more accurately than target conventions, yet receiving choices do not consistently agree with the sender's convention.
