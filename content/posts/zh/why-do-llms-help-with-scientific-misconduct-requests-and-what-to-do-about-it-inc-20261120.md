作者: Almene De Meran Meguimtsop, Daniel E. Acuna

Large language models (LLMs) support scientific research, but some of that assistance can also undermine research integrity. Existing safety evaluations in science often measure whether models refuse misconduct requests, but rarely explain whether they even recognize the problem or why they comply.
