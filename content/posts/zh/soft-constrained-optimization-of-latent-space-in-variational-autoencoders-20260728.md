# Soft-Constrained Optimization of Latent Space in Variational Autoencoders

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23751v1)

作者: Ye Shi

The usefulness of a variational autoencoder (VAE) depends on two properties of its latent space that are hard to obtain together: high encoding capacity in the individual latent variables, and a low-dimensional, disentangled organization of those variables. Weakening the Kullback-Leibler regularization raises capacity but degrades disentanglement, while strengthening it prunes latent variables away entirely. We formulate VAE training as a soft-constrained optimization problem that addresses both
