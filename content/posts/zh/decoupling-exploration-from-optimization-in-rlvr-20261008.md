# Decoupling Exploration from Optimization in RLVR

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10536v1)

作者: Saif Punjwani, Micah Goldblum

Modern language models undergo reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) on top of already-trained checkpoints. A key promise of RLVR is the discovery of new reasoning strategies. In principle, a model can sample novel ideas absent from its prior training data. In practice, however, augmenting RLVR with strong novelty incentives has seen limited success and can degrade model quality.
