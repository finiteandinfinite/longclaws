# SocietyBench: Forecasting Counterfactual Social-World Evolution

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04009v1)

作者: Zhenran Wang, Zhonghan Bian, Jinsong Li 等

Large language models (LLMs), and the agents built on top of them, are now benchmarked heavily on whether they can finish a task -- fix a bug, drive a browser, operate a GUI. A complementary social ability, namely how well a model understands and forecasts the way real social events unfold, has barely been measured. We introduce SocietyBench, an end-to-end benchmark that takes a one-line event topic, collects Web news and social-media posts across five platforms, distills them into a date-indexe
