# Definitions, Label Names, or Demonstrations? Auditing What Governs LLM Annotation

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09457v1)

作者: Viraj Mishra, Shubham Gupta, Dhruv Gupta 等

Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly used to annotate social-science texts, but high accuracy does not establish that they apply researcher-supplied category definitions: predictions may instead reflect prior associations with category labels or labeled demonstrations. We introduce a counterfactual audit that reassigns definitions among output labels, distinguishing adherence to supplied definitions from preservation of a dataset's original mapping.
