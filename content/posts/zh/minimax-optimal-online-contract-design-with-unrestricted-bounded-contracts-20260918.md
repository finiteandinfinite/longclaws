# Minimax-Optimal Online Contract Design with Unrestricted Bounded Contracts

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20353v1)

作者: Rui Ai, David Simchi-Levi, Han Zhong

We study repeated contract design when a principal observes outcomes but not the actions that generate them. The principal may use any bounded outcome-contingent payment vector, and the agent's best response can make expected profit discontinuous in those payments. For every fixed number $m\ge2$ of outcomes, the minimax regret over $T$ rounds is of order $T^{m/(m+1)}$, up to logarithmic factors.
