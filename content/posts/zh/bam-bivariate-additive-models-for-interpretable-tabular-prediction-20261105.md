# BAM: Bivariate Additive Models for Interpretable Tabular Prediction

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05940v1)

作者: Atsushi Koike

Generalized Additive Models with pairwise interactions (GA$^2$Ms) are regarded as glass-box models, since each pairwise shape function can be visualized as a contour plot. In practice, the shape functions learned by methods such as EBM or NODE-GAM are so complex (irregular contours and sharp local fluctuations) that a practitioner cannot meaningfully interpret them or check them against domain knowledge.
