# VIALS: A Benchmark for Visual Interpretation of Artifacts in the Life Sciences

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21357v1)

作者: Elaine Lau, Thanuka Udumulla, Lee Izhaki-Tavor 等

In professional life sciences workflows, scientists routinely interpret visual artifacts (gel blots, microscopy images, plasmid maps, flow cytometry plots, molecular structures, ...) to inform research decisions. We introduce VIALS, a visual question-answering benchmark with 161 such interpretation tasks, spanning the types of artifacts examined throughout experimental workflows in the biotech industry (rather than polished figures from publications and textbooks).
