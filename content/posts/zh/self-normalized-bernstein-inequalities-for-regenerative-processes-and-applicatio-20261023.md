# Self-normalized Bernstein Inequalities for Regenerative Processes and Applications to Learning Without Independence

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30119v1)

作者: Imon Banerjee, Pierre Alquier

We prove a self-normalized Bernstein inequality for bounded functions of a regenerative process with independent, identically distributed marked cycles of finite mean length. The bound uses observed cycle lengths and retains explicit terms for truncation and the cycle ending after the observation horizon. Selecting a tuning parameter from a finite deterministic collection gives a version of Bernstein inequality where all terms are empirical but one, related to the tails of the regeneration times
