# When Should a Voice Agent Speak? End-of-Turn Detection in Turkish

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25489v1)

作者: Ahmet Tuğrul Bayrak, Fatma Nur Korkmaz, Mustafa Sertaç Türkel 等

A voice agent that waits for a fixed silence before replying either interrupts users who pause mid-utterance or responds late to users who have finished. This study examines the decision at the onset of silence, using turn ends and turn-internal pauses extracted from the Real-TurnTurk corpus of Turkish dyadic video calls. A fine-tuned Turkish TurnGPT model, a zero-shot Turkish language model and a deployed multilingual detector are compared, together with voice-activity timing and facial feature
