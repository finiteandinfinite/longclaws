# DiaCap: Jointly Learning Who Speaks When for Audiovisual Dialogue Captioning

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33153v1)

作者: Binfei Ji, Zhiwen Cao, Anqi Yang 等

We present DiaCap, an audiovisual LLM for comprehensive dialogue captioning, which produces the transcript, timestamps, and a visual description of the speaker for every utterance in a video. Considering that utterance timing and audiovisual diarization are tightly coupled, DiaCap unifies the tasks in a single model, trained in two complementary stages.
