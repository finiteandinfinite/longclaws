# Text-to-Image Generative Modeling Without Language Pretraining

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26863v1)

作者: Keshigeyan Chandrasegaran, Yue Zhao, Armin W. Thomas 等

Modern text-to-image (T2I) systems use internet-scale language pretraining for text-conditioned image generation. We ask whether language pretraining is necessary for strong T2I generation. We introduce Halcyon, a family of early-fusion multimodal autoregressive models pretrained entirely from scratch on GPIC, a dataset of 100M image-text pairs. Halcyon-0.52B outperforms LlamaGen-XL/8 (48.0 vs. 63.3 gFD) with 18.2$\times$ less pretraining compute. Further, Halcyon-1.6B outperforms JiT-T2I-XXL/8+
