# Training Reasoning Models in Simulation to Natively Use Real Robots

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18562v1)

作者: Jiaming Tang, Zhuoyang Zhang, Junxian Guo 等

Robot-use agents (RUAs) have emerged as a promising approach to general-purpose robot control. However, strong performance has so far been achieved only by frontier proprietary models such as GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable 5.2, whereas open-weight models remain substantially less capable. We show that an open-weight 27B reasoning model can be trained to use real robots natively through reinforcement learning, entirely in simulation and without demonstrations.
