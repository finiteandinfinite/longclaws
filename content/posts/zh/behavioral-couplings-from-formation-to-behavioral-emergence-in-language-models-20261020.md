# Behavioral Couplings: From Formation to Behavioral Emergence in Language Models

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23847v1)

作者: Dongyoon Hahm, Woogyeol Jin, Dennis Wei 等

Fine-tuning large language models (LLMs) can induce behaviors beyond those explicitly targeted. For example, training a model to respond more warmly can make it more sycophantic, and training it to write insecure code can cause broad misalignment on prompts unrelated to coding.
