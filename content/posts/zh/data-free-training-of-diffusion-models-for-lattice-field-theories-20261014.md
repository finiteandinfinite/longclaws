# Data Free Training of Diffusion Models for Lattice Field Theories

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18863v1)

作者: Dennis Grinwald, Ankur Singha, Khaled Kahouli 等

Sampling equilibrium field configurations remains a major computational bottleneck in lattice field theory, motivating the development of generative samplers that can produce independent configurations. Diffusion models are promising for this purpose, but their standard denoising score matching objective requires samples from the target distribution, seemingly reintroducing the original sampling problem. Here, we show that diffusion models can instead be trained from their own generated samples
