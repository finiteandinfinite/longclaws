# Compression Through a Context Lens: Evaluating Adaptation, Quantization, and Distillation in Arabic Legal LLMs

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07988v1)

作者: Md. Rafiul Biswas, George Mikros, Wajdi Zaghouani

Model compression can facilitate the deployment of large language models in resource-constrained legal settings, but its effects on domain-specific performance may depend on the evaluation protocol. We investigate this issue using ArabLegalEval, a Saudi Arabic legal benchmark, under both with-context and no-context evaluation. The former provides supporting legal passages, while the latter assesses performance without external context. Removing the context produces accuracy gaps of up to 27 perc
