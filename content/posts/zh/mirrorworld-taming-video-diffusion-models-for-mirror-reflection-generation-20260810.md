# MirrorWorld: Taming Video Diffusion Models for Mirror Reflection Generation

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07463v1)

作者: Youjun Zhao, Alex Warren, Gary K. L. Tam 等

Recent advances in video diffusion models (VDMs) have enabled high-fidelity video synthesis. However, generating mirror reflections remains challenging because the content within a mirror must remain consistent with the surrounding scene. Existing VDMs are not specifically designed to model scene-to-mirror relationships, which can lead to reflections with incorrect content or inconsistent spatial arrangements.
