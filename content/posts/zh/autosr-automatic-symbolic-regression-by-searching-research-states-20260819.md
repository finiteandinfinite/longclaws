> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16876v1)

作者: Kejia Zhang, Youran Sun, Xinyu Ren 等

We introduce Automatic Symbolic Regression (AutoSR), a fully automated system that instantiates Research-Space Symbolic Regression by searching persistent scientific investigations rather than isolated equations.
