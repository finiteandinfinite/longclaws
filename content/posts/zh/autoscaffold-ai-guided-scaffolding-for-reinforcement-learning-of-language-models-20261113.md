作者: Zijian Zhang, Rizhen Hu, Jiangweizhi Peng 等

Training-time scaffolds, such as strategies or hints added to the prompt during training, offer a natural way to turn this information into guidance, but their design and adaptation are largely left to humans and tailored to specific domains.
