# Truthful Calibration Measures for Sequential Prediction

> 来源: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21348v1)

作者: Anagha Gokul, Jason Hartline, Lunjia Hu 等

Calibration requires probabilistic reports to be conditionally unbiased and reliably interpretable as probabilities. A calibration measure assigns numerical error to miscalibrated reports. Haghtalab et al. (2024) proposed an approximately truthful calibration measure for online prediction, leaving open whether exact truthfulness is compatible with completeness and soundness. We resolve this question negatively for sequential binary prediction: exact truthfulness is incompatible with completeness
